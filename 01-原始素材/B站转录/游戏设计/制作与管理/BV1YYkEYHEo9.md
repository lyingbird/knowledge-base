The first thing the script does is pick a random room from the top right and makes this the entrance. It will then randomly place a room to the left, right, or beneath this room. This process is repeated from room to room, and if the path hits the edge of the level, it also goes down until we get to the lowest level. When it tries to go down once more, an exit is made.
